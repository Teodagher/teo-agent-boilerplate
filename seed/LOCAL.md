# Local rules

Your own rules and improvements to how you work, on top of `AGENTS.md`. `AGENTS.md`, the
template's skills and `tools/` are replaced on every template update; this file, your own
skills in `skills/` and your memory are yours and are never touched by an update.

Write one rule per line, newest last, and log each change as a decision
(`python3 tools/memory.py log decision "..."`).
